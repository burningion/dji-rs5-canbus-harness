UWB connection layout & first setup+
Tag on subject→ UWB →Anchor + camera ESP32→ radio →RS5 ESP32→ CAN →RS5

The RS5 ESP32 is the main controller. Mouse and PS4 control work with the camera node off. Once paired, the camera node supplies UWB measurements when available; select your tag and wait for TAG LIVE to add follow. Detection never starts movement. The body ESP32 stays connected to Control Desk by USB.

Mount the anchor so it turns with the lens, with its measurement plane horizontal. Keep the camera approximately level. This kit tracks horizontal bearing, not subject height. A handle-mounted anchor needs a different controller.

Identify pads by silkscreen and the Makerfabs V1.1 schematic, not a viewing-angle pin count. UART is 3.3 V, 115200 8N1. Leave Feather TX/GPIO39 and anchor RXD1 unconnected. The camera node has its own power; no UART or ground cable runs to the body. Follow the repository's wireless power and pairing guide. Keep RS5 accessory VCC insulated. For the original wired setup, the anchor UART and ground connect directly to the body Feather instead.

Keep the Makerfabs factory firmware. Pair one tag in AOA System over the anchor’s USB-NATIVE port and select JSON output (USER_CMD 0, then SAVE). Enter the received short tag ID above and apply settings. Wait for TAG LIVE before pressing X. Preview mode uses tag 1234.

Start at 5°/s with the tag 2–5 m ahead and slightly off center. Confirm the camera turns toward it; stop and reverse follow direction if it moves away. Use center offset for mounting alignment. Tag loss, leaving this window, or connection loss stops follow; reacquire and press X again. Readings must arrive at least every 300 ms. Keep the tag within ±55° and 0.75–20 m.

Connect a PS4 controller+

Use a USB data cable, or hold SHARE + PS until the light flashes and pair DUALSHOCK 4 Wireless Controller in your Mac’s Bluetooth settings.

Open this page in Chrome or Edge, click the page, then press a controller button. The controller is detected automatically. Release L1 once after connecting.

Hold L1 + left stick to pan and tilt. Release L1 to stop. Mouse controls work whenever you hold them; no mode switch is needed. Reverse an axis above if its direction feels wrong.

Session details+

Waiting for a device.

The first control you hold takes priority until released. Release both before switching devices. Leaving this window, losing the connection, or changing settings stops movement; release and hold again to continue. Space, Escape, or Circle also stops. Keep the gimbal’s power control accessible.
